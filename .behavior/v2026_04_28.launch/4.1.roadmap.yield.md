## dependency order

```
phase 0 (deps)
    ↓
phase 1 (domain.objects)
    ↓
phase 2 (transformers) ←── depends on domain.objects
    ↓
phase 3 (communicators) ←── independent of transformers
    ↓
phase 4 (orchestrators) ←── depends on transformers + communicators
    ↓
phase 5 (contract/sdk) ←── depends on orchestrators
    ↓
phase 6 (acceptance) ←── depends on contract/sdk
```

phases 2 and 3 can run in parallel after phase 1.
