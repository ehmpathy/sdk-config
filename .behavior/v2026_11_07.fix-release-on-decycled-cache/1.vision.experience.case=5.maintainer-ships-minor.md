# c5 — the maintainer ships the break; a `!` header is refused (sharp × criti)

## narrative

the maintainer wants adopters on `^0.3.0` NOT to auto-take the break. they title the pr `feat(cache): bind the cache contract to with-simple-cache 0.17 (iso-time)` and name the break in the body. after approval they squash-merge; the release job reads `feat(...)` and opens the 0.4.0 release pr. they see 0.4.0, not 0.3.1, and know caret adopters stay on 0.3.x.

the sharp edge: a habit from elsewhere — `feat(cache)!:` — would miss the release regex and mint a silent patch. this repo refuses it first: commitlint (`commitlint.config.js`, `subject-exclamation-mark: never`) fails `test:commits` in ci on any `!` header, and the config's own comment teaches the fix: "use break: instead of feat!:".

## fail-safe

- fast: `test:commits` runs on the pr, before merge.
- loud: ci red, `✖ found 1 problems` from commitlint.
- teaches the fix: the rule's comment names `break:`; the type enum lists the legal headers.
