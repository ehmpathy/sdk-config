## the four quadrants — all four demoed

per `define.experience._.demo.surf-school`, feel × care has four quadrants:

|  | **happy** | **sharp** |
|---|---|---|
| **critipath** | `case=1` · `case=3` · `case=7` · 🔴 `case=8` · 🔴 `case=9` | `case=2` · `case=4` · `case=5` |
| **alterpath** | `case=6` · 🔴 `h2`/`h3` (itemized) | `case=4` `[t3]` |

🔴 **the two late critipaths are both HAPPY, and that is why they were missed.** every axis the
first walk found was discovered through a **refusal** — an absent org, a malformed one, a store
miss. F and G raise no error at all: they vary what a call that **succeeds** returns. ⇒ a walk that
hunts for failure modes is blind to a correct-but-wrong-value experience, and both blockers `r5`
raised sit in exactly that blind spot.

🔴 **`case=2` crossed the feel axis on F8** — from happy (*"no experience occurred"*) to sharp
(*"the config refuses to boot"*). the happy-critipath quadrant is one lighter and the
sharp-critipath quadrant one heavier, which is the quadrant-level shape of the same trade the
verdict makes everywhere: **fewer quiet paths, more loud ones.**

the **sharp alterpath** is a **blank declared org** — `"organization": "   "`, which trims to empty
and throws. it hits the same refusal edge as `case=4`, at lower care: the author is at the keyboard,
holds the value in hand, and recovers with a real one in the next keystroke. no fallback is lost.

🔴 **its subject moved on F11, and what moved is instructive.** it was a malformed **override** —
`genGetConfig({ org: '   ' })` — and it was promoted from *itemized* to *demoed* on exactly one
distinct assertion: that the refusal names **which source** supplied the bad value, so a caller is
not sent to edit a config field they never wrote.

⇒ **that assertion had no root of its own.** it existed because a second source existed. one source
returns the cell to the blank-config case it was promoted away from — the same refusal, one remedy,
and no collapse in axis B left to hide anything.

⇒ 🟡 **the honest read: a degree of freedom manufactured the distinct experience that justified
demoing it.** the demo was correct while the freedom stood, and it is the clearest instance of what
`ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-ehmpathy#674` names — a freedom pays for itself in obligations it created.

### 🟡 the method lesson, and it is a SECOND one

the dimensions file already records the lesson F/G/H taught: the walk took `A` (actor) as the only
axis about **who holds a value**, and never asked which artifact each value lives in.

🔴 **I, J, and K share a different root, and it is the one that survived that fix.** the walk
modelled every value as **read or absent** — a binary — and never as **read, then transformed**:

| axis | the transformation the walk skipped |
|---|---|
| **I** | `repo` is RESOLVED — from an override, or from a file that may refuse |
| **J** | `org` is NORMALIZED — trimmed, then splice-checked |
| **K** | `keyPath` is ASSEMBLED — from how keys nest, and may come out empty |

⇒ **three values, three transformations, three axes** — and each transformation has its own failure
surface the read/absent binary cannot express.

⚠️ **all three had a green test and no cell, and all three recurred once unfixed.** the recurrence

   a build that strips the field before `safeParse` keeps every other test green and breaks every
   consumer that reads its own org. it ranks here on **cost**, never on density.
8. 🔴 **`case=8`** (org varies per choice) — the per-fill read, clamped. it ranks last because the
   experience is narrow (two choice files, one process, one org at a time) — and it is a critipath
   because the hazard is a **plausible refactor**, not a user mistake: a later author hoists the org
   to factory time to match `repo`, and silently re-points every derived path.
