## .the gap — one grades the STATE, the other the ACT

| rule | its subject | its question |
|---|---|---|
| `forbid.combinatorial-explosion` (`#674`) | the **state** — the space is too large | *"who asked for this, and what breaks without it?"* |
| **this** | the **act over time** — the space GREW and nobody shrank it | *"which freedoms still have a live demand?"* |

🔴 **`#674` alone guards the DOOR and never the ROOM.** it fires when a freedom enters, and it is
silent the day after. so a codebase can pass it on every single commit and still accrete — because
each key entered against a demand that was real *at the time*, and no rule ever asks whether that
demand survived.

⇒ the asymmetry is the whole argument:

- a degree of freedom enters by the hand of **someone with a reason**
- it exits by the hand of **no one**, because a cut is a break with no author
- ⇒ accretion is the **default**, and a bar at the door does not reverse a default. it only slows it

## .the claim

> **degrees of freedom accrete unless actively eliminated. a freedom whose named demand no longer
> exists is a defect on the day that demand dies — not on the day someone notices.**

so the rule mandates an **elimination pass**, and it charges the freedom rather than the cut:

| today's default | the rule's |
|---|---|
| *"a cut would be a break — who needs that?"* | *"what still demands it? name the consumer."* |

## .the measured case — the same diff `#674` was seeded from

`ehmpathy/sdk-config` `v2026_09_08.feat-org-scoped-param-derive`. one optional key, `org?`, on
`genGetConfig`.

🔴 **it passed `#674`'s own test at the moment it entered.** when the vision was authored the org
had no required source, so an override was the *only* way to pin one — a named demand, and a real
one.

then `F8` landed: `organization` became a **required** config field. ⇒ **the demand died that day.**

- the key survived `F8` and every round after it
- five peer reviewers read the diff and none raised it
- the wisher caught it, from outside, in one sentence:
  > *"why wouldn't we just eliminate this nasty codebranch / and force users to say organization
  > instead / why would it be absent / if we require it"*

⇒ **the freedom outlived its reason by the entire execution stage**, and the mechanism was that
nobody ever re-asked the entry question. `#674` cannot catch this, by construction — it grades the
addition, and the addition was justified.

## .the shape of the fix

a new rule in `ehmpathy/role=architect`, the peer of `#674`:

```
briefs/practices/…/rule.forbid.freedom-degrees-accretion.md
```

### when it fires — every trigger is a CHANGE OF GROUND, never an addition

| trigger | what to re-ask |
|---|---|
| a value becomes **required** | every override, default, and fallback that existed to supply it |
| a fallback lands | the override that existed because there was none |
| two options collapse to one | the flag that picked between them |
| a consumer is deleted | the freedom that consumer alone demanded |
| a rule or verdict narrows the space | every freedom the wider space justified |

⇒ **the trigger is never *"time passed"*.** it is a change that invalidates a prior demand, and
each one is a diff someone authored — so the pass has a home: the same change that kills the
demand kills the freedom.

### the test

> **name the consumer who demands this today. not the one who demanded it when it entered.**

- a live consumer, named → it stays
- the original demand is dead and no new one replaced it → 🔴 **cut it in the change that killed it**
- *"someone might still depend on it"* → measure. an unmeasured population is not a consumer

### the counter-bound

- ⚠️ it must NOT become a licence to break published contracts on a whim — the cut is owed at the
  moment the demand dies, and the *release grade* of that cut is a separate question
- a freedom with a **measured** live consumer is not accretion, however old
- a freedom retained for a stated forward reason, recorded, is not accretion — it is a decision
- 🔴 and it must not fire on the `#674` axis: a key that entered with no demand is **that** rule's,
  never this one's. this rule's subject is a key that HAD one

### enforcement

- **blocker**: a change that makes a value required, a fallback authoritative, or an option
  singular, and does not cut the freedoms that existed to supply the old state
- **blocker**: a degree of freedom retained past its named demand, with no new consumer named
- **nitpick**: a freedom whose demand was never named at all — which is `#674`'s catch, surfaced
  by this rule's pass
